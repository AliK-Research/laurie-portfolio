Stories on Wattpad

Online fiction · Serialized novels

2014–2019

Between 2014 and 2019, Laurie wrote and serialized a series of standalone novels on Wattpad, building a following of over 10,000 readers. The demand for regular updates required her to publish new chapters weekly, strengthening her writing skills, discipline, and passion for novel writing. Though she has since outgrown the genre that defined her early work and no longer maintains the account, the experience marked a formative chapter in her development as a writer.
