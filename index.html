Chapter I

Prologue

I started writing because I could not stop noticing things: the way my grandmother folded letters in thirds, the silence after a train leaves, the word people almost say before they choose a kinder one. I am a creative writing student, and most days I am trying to put those small things down before they leave.

I write short fiction, poetry, and the kind of essay that begins as one thing and ends as another.1 I like quiet stories with a crack running through them, and poems that sound good read aloud in an empty kitchen.

This site is a small book of what I have made so far. Read it in order, or open it anywhere.

Chapter III

On Craft

Voice
Intimate first person, close third, and the occasional second person when a story needs to look the reader in the eye.
Form
Short story, flash fiction, lyric and narrative poetry, the personal essay, prose poems.
Revision
I cut until it hurts, then put one sentence back.
Workshop
Giving and taking critique with care, editing peers' manuscripts, reading aloud.
Editing
Copyediting, line editing, and editorial work on a student literary magazine.
Languages
English and French; I like the gaps between them most.
