I

A Note on the Writer

Born and raised in Québec City, Laurie Legault is an emerging English writer. Bilingual in French and English, she grew up on the Valcartier military base and attended the Dollard-des-Ormeaux English school, where she gained her writing confidence through yearly nominations for poetry slams.

Later, she began writing fiction on an online platform and gained over ten thousand readers, prompting her to pursue her writing more seriously. By the time she graduated high school, Laurie won the Quebec Chronicle Telegraph English Prize award and went on to complete a DEC in languages.

Today, Laurie lives in Montréal and is a third-year creative writing major with a minor in professional writing. She continues to expand and polish her narrative voice through a collection of short stories, one of which, Sandhill Cranes, has been featured in Humber College’s student-run literature and art magazine, Arrival. In her pieces, Laurie embraces her Québécois roots and explores themes of anger, grief, nostalgia, denial, and love, while continuing to draw inspiration from her and her family’s lives. She hopes to pursue a career in fiction writing and novel editing.
