I woke up one day, and the world continued without me

A collection of short stories · In progress

A debut collection of short stories written throughout Laurie's university years. Bringing together her earliest drafts, this soon-to-be-published work marks her first achievement toward becoming a published author.

Honouring her Québecois roots, these stories explore themes of anger, grief, nostalgia, denial, and love; they look back on life as it once was, the imperfections that define us, and our longing to be understood. At their heart lies the search for connection in a world that can feel vast, lonely, and indifferent.
